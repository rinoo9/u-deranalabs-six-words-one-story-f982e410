# Six Words, One Story

Six-word story entry for the make.fun bounty "Six Words, One Story". The program `main.py` writes exactly six words to `/output/entry.txt` — "Grandma's recipe died with her. Almost." — a complete story in miniature: a grandmother's legacy seems lost with her, until the final word reverses the loss and leaves the reader to fill in who remembers the recipe.
